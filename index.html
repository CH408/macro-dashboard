How to read the labels. Confirmed a number taken directly from the named public source. Inferred a judgment from simple rules; the reasoning is shown. Unknown no reliable data this run. The call is one of four states (growth rising or falling, inflation rising or falling) plus a five-step scale from risk-off (investors avoiding risk) to risk-on (investors seeking risk). Changes are percent for prices and indexes, and plain differences (in percentage points) for rates and yields.
